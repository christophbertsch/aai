**Preserve the person. Continue the conversation.**  
Legacy.AI is a mobile-first platform for recording and preserving a person’s voice, wisdom, and personality through a structured video interview experience. The app transforms life stories into searchable archives and creates a conversational AI that can speak and respond like the original person — even after they’re gone.

---

## 🧠 Features

- 🎙️ **Modular Legacy Interview**
  - Choose between 100 (Silver), 200 (Gold), or 300 (Forever) life questions
  - One-question-at-a-time interface with video capture and review
  - Save and resume interviews across multiple sessions

- 🧬 **AI Personality Builder**
  - Optional personality quiz based on the Mini-IPIP (Big Five traits)
  - Shapes the emotional tone and response style of the user’s AI model

- 📤 **Real-Time Upload & Processing**
  - Video and transcript uploaded per question upon approval
  - Transcription, semantic indexing, and voice cloning initiated automatically

- 📚 **Personal Q&A Vault**
  - Searchable archive of video answers
  - Filter by theme, emotion, or life stage

- 🤖 **Conversational AI Integration**
  - Interact with a person’s AI via text, voice, or even WhatsApp
  - Hear the person speak in their own voice with context-aware responses
